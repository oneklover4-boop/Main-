Four Focused Offers

Launch Health Check

A structured assessment of your launch readiness: stakeholder interviews and clear recommendations for your team. We pinpoint the gaps that matter most and prioritise them, so your team knows exactly where to focus first.

Launch Strategy Support

Support to get the right strategic imperatives, critical success factors, positioning and stakeholder priorities that shape your launch roadmap. We work alongside your team to turn that strategy into a clear, actionable plan everyone can align behind.

Launch Readiness Programme

A readiness framework with scorecards, workshops and structured gap closure. Regular check-ins keep momentum up and hold every function accountable to the same launch timeline.

Launch Office Support

Fractional launch leadership support: decision management and day-to-day cross-functional launch planning coordination. We act as an extension of your team, keeping plans on track and surfacing risks before they become delays.

Case Study

Client Example Request

A mid-sized biopharma company approaching its first commercial launch in years engaged Launch Doctors for a Launch Health Check roughly 24 months ahead of anticipated approval. The assessment surfaced gaps in cross-functional alignment between medical, market access and commercial teams, along with unclear ownership across key launch milestones. The resulting recommendations informed an Integrated Launch Plan, giving leadership a single, governed view of the path to launch.
