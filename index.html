About

Design systems thinking, applied to real products.

I started as a visual designer, moved into product design, and picked up front-end engineering along the way so I could see my own work through to production. That path shapes how I work now: I sweat the details of a component in Figma, then I sweat the same details in the browser.

Most of my work lives at the intersection of design systems and product UX — building the shared language a team uses to move fast without losing consistency, then applying it to the highest-leverage screens in the product.

  • Design systems & component libraries
  • Complex dashboards & data-dense UI
  • Accessible, production-grade front-end code
